Diwali Firecracker Game – Gaon ka Aangan

शुभ दीपावली! Happy Diwali!

A free first-person 3D Diwali game you play in your browser. Walk through a small Indian village at night, light diyas around a rangoli courtyard and burst firecrackers with real sounds, then look up for a drone show and a grand fireworks finale. No download, no smoke, no pollution.

Crackers you can burst

  • Phuljhadi (sparklers) and pencil, held in your hand
  • Anar (flower pot) and chakri (ground spinner)
  • Rockets and sky shots that burst in the sky
  • Laxmi bomb, sutli bomb, ladi and saanp goli
  • An akash kandil lantern to hang, and diyas to light

How to play

Use a laptop or desktop. Move with W A S D and look with the mouse. Pick a cracker with 1 to 0 or Q, place it with F and light it with E. Press M to mute and H for help. Turn the sound on for the full festival.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, completely free in any modern desktop browser with WebGL.

Does it work on phones?

Phones get a Happy Diwali card with a short gameplay video and a button to copy the link for your laptop.

Is it safe for kids?

Yes, it is a safe and pollution-free way to enjoy Diwali fireworks.
